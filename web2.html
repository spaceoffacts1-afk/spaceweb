20 Fascinating Space Facts

Solar System (6)

  1. The Sun accounts for 99.8% of the total mass in our solar system.
  2. Venus is the hottest planet, not Mercury, due to its thick atmosphere.
  3. Jupiter's "Great Red Spot" is a storm that has raged for over 300 years.
  4. Neptune has the fastest wind speeds in the solar system, reaching 1,600 mph.
  5. Saturn's density is so low it could float in water.
  6. Olympus Mons on Mars is the largest volcano, 3x higher than Everest.

Planets (4)

  1. A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus.
  2. Some planets, like Jupiter and Saturn, may experience diamond rain.
  3. Earth's moon is bigger than the dwarf planet Pluto.
  4. Uranus rotates on its side, unlike any other planet.

Other Celestial Bodies (10)

  1. There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on all Earth's beaches.
  2. Space is completely silent because there is no air to carry sound.
  3. Black holes have gravity so strong that even light cannot escape.
  4. The Boomerang Nebula is the coldest natural place in the universe.
  5. Rogue planets wander through space without orbiting any star.
  6. Neutron stars are so dense one teaspoon weighs as much as humanity.
  7. Galaxies are constantly colliding and reshaping over billions of years.
  8. Space junk: over 500,000 pieces of debris orbit the Earth.
  9. A "shooting star" is actually just a tiny piece of space dust burning up.
  10. The Milky Way galaxy is roughly 105,700 light-years wide.
